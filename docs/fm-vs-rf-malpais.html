The arroyo, where the models diverge

Malpais Arroyo, San Juan Basin · both models held this reach out · Sentinel-2 2020

0.889FM riparian AUROC

A narrow desert wash — the one morphology unlike the wide-river reaches the models trained on. The per-pixel RF is near-chance here (0.557); the foundation model, reading spatial context, lifts it to 0.889. That gap is the whole reason the FM earns its keep.

On the map: green = FM predicted riparian, orange = RF predicted riparian, blue outline = NMRipMap truth. Watch the RF scatter onto dry ground while the FM tracks the corridor.

Why the FM looks blocky

OlmoEarth classifies in 2×2-pixel patches, so its extent is coarser-grained than the RF's per-pixel map — a rendering artifact of the patch head, not a quality difference. Read: the full result.
