The arroyo, where the foundation model earns its keep

Malpais Arroyo, San Juan Basin · both models held this reach out · Sentinel-2 2020

0.889FM riparian AUROC
0.557RF riparian AUC

A narrow desert wash — the one morphology unlike the wide-river reaches the models trained on. On a scored leave-one-reach-out transfer, the per-pixel RF is near-chance (0.557) while the foundation model, reading spatial context, reaches 0.889.

On the map (over the NMRipMap-labeled reach): green = FM predicted riparian, orange = RF predicted riparian, blue outline = NMRipMap truth. The RF fires only in the wetter eastern end — where the spectra resemble the river reaches it trained on — and misses the western dry wash that the FM tracks. Genuine under-prediction (~0.1% of pixels, verified against a fully-covered cube), and the 0.557-vs-0.889 gap made spatial.

Note on the RF layer: an earlier version of this map used a partial RF export (~1/5 of the reach) and was withdrawn as an invalid comparison. This uses the regenerated full-extent RF prediction (reproduces AUROC 0.561 vs the documented 0.557). The catch, and the lesson.
