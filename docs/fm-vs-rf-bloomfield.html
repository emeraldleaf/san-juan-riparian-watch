The deployable map — Bloomfield

San Juan River at Bloomfield · a reach neither model trained on · Sentinel-2 2020

Both models were fit on the four labeled reaches, then asked to map riparian extent over this unseen reach — the actual deployment task. The foundation model won the decision (leave-one-reach-out macro AUROC 0.872 vs 0.798), so this is the FM's map, shown against the RF baseline and the NMRipMap truth.

On the map: green = FM predicted riparian, orange = RF predicted riparian, blue outline = NMRipMap truth. On a well-sampled river like this the two agree closely — the FM's edge shows on hard morphologies (see the arroyo).
