Invasive extent over time

San Juan River at Farmington · tamarisk + Russian olive (combined) · Landsat TM/ETM+ · exploratory

An attempt to map the invasive footprint back through the Landsat record, one sensor family (Landsat-5/7, no OLI). The bars below are 5-year window composites — but treat them as exploratory (see the warning).

⚠ Not a robust result. Follow-up checks showed this reconstruction is unreliable before ~2000: the pure-Landsat-5 1990 value swings ~1 pp with the compositing recipe no matter what normalization is applied, so no trajectory shape can be claimed. The reliable, label-validated product is the present-day corridor-vs-invasive map (23% of the corridor is invasive). Full story: the write-up.
