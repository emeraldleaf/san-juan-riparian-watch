The corridor, and the invaders within it

San Juan River at Farmington · 2020 · Landsat, NMRipMap-labelled

23%of the mapped riparian corridor is invasive
tamarisk / Russian olive

A watershed manager doesn't just want "where is the green corridor" — they want how much of it is the wrong plant. Green is the predicted riparian-woody corridor (7.6 km²); red is the invasive share within it (1.7 km²).

In-sample calibration (not independent validation): the 23% masked prediction reproduces the NMRipMap label proportion (15,656 / 67,625 woody pixels invasive = 23%) — the model is well-calibrated to the 2020 labels it was trained on. A spatially-held-out test is the proper next step. Invasive is masked to inside the corridor (the two classifiers are independent).
